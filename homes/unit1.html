About

Live steps from the best of South Oceanside in this bright, brand-new 2-bedroom, 2-bath home, built in 2023. Walking distance to the beach and to South O's restaurants, coffee shops, and bars.

The unit gets great natural light. The kitchen has all-new appliances: refrigerator, oven, microwave and dishwasher. Full-size washer and dryer in the unit, central A/C, and heat pump. The primary bedroom has an ensuite bathroom, and both bedrooms have walk-in closets.

Outside, you have your own private entrance, patio, and yard area. The unit is fully detached; the owners live in the separate front house on the same lot. Two dedicated off-street parking spots are included, which is a real perk this close to the coast.

Available furnished or unfurnished. Pets negotiable.
