About

A private 550 sq ft studio on the same South Oceanside lot, a block from Coast Highway and a short walk to the beach and South O's restaurants, coffee shops, and bars.

The studio has its own kitchen, bathroom, and in-unit laundry, with a mini-split for A/C and heat. Two private entrances open onto your own outdoor space and yard area, home to a big avocado tree and a lemon tree.

One dedicated off-street parking spot is included, and gas, electricity, water, trash, and sewer are covered in the rent. The owners live in the front house on the same lot.

Available furnished or unfurnished. Pets negotiable.
